About DataPACT

DataPACT is a European Horizon Europe project developing innovative tools and methodologies that embed compliance, ethics, and environmental sustainability into data and AI pipelines from the ground up. It supports organizations in building trustworthy, transparent, and regulation-aware data workflows. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

Through a comprehensive Compliance Toolbox, Framework, and Pipeline management tools, DataPACT accelerates the creation of compliant data operations, reduces time-to-market, and fosters a secure and sustainable digital environment. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}
